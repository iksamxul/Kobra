BEFORE YOU PRESS PLAY.
What is Kobra?+

A local-first video player for Windows, with a quiet liquid-glass interface, everyday viewing tools, and a connected set of creative workspaces in Kobra Studio.

What's the difference between Kobra and Kobra Studio?+

Kobra is for everyday watching: Home, Watch, My videos and Tools. Kobra Studio adds media review, an editor, projects, research, writing, study, delivery and advanced playback controls. Both modes share the same player and your files.

Can I edit videos in Kobra?+

Yes, simple cuts. Kobra Studio's Editor arranges clips from your videos into one edit and renders an MP4 on your device, up to 30 minutes long. The rendering engine (FFmpeg) downloads once, the first time you render. Your source files are never changed.

Does it play every video format?+

Playback depends on the codecs supported by the app's Chromium engine. Some local files can use an optional FFmpeg compatibility conversion, with a 512 MB input limit. Kobra does not claim universal codec support or full PotPlayer parity.

Can I use it without an account?+

Yes. Your local videos and workspaces don't need a Kobra account. Network streams and the first optional compatibility-core download need an internet connection.
