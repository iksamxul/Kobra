A few good questions.

Before you press play

What is Kobra?+

A local-first video player for Windows, with a quiet glass interface, everyday viewing tools, and a connected set of creative workspaces in Kobra Studio.

What’s the difference between Kobra and Kobra Studio?+

Kobra is for everyday watching: Home, Watch, My videos and Tools. Kobra Studio adds Media Studio, the Editor, Projects, Research, Writing, Study, Delivery and Playback Lab. Both modes share the same player and your files.

Does it play every video format?+

Playback depends on the codecs supported by the app’s Chromium engine. Some local files can use an optional FFmpeg compatibility conversion, with a 512 MB input limit. Kobra does not claim universal codec support.

Can I use it without an account?+

Yes. Your local videos and workspaces don’t need a Kobra account. Network streams and the first optional compatibility download need an internet connection.
